💡 Platform Features & FAQ

How does the AI Journey Planner work?

Type or record your request (e.g., "Take me from Coimbatore Junction to Chennai Central"). Our engine will compute and suggest multi-modal options: Fastest (Cab direct), Cheapest (Bus direct), and Eco-friendly (Train route).

What happens when I trigger the SOS?

A continuous alarm siren starts playing and alerts are immediately sent to the police (112) and your trusted contacts. Your live location telemetry is logged on the admin console. Re-click the SOS button to stop the alarm.

How do I earn Eco-Reward points?

Plan your journey using low-carbon modes (trains, buses, electric vehicles). For every trip logged, points are awarded based on carbon savings versus a single-occupancy car. You can redeem 500 points for bus ticket coupons.
